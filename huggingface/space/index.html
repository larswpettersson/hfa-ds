STPA workbenchControl structure, unsafe control actions, loss scenarios and safety constraints. HTA + TTAHierarchical task analysis with human error identification per task. BowtieHazard, top event, causes, barriers and consequences.

AI analysis

Open a tool, press AI (STPA: the panel on the right) and pick a provider under Settings. For Hugging Face, create a fine-grained token with Make calls to Inference Providers at huggingface.co/settings/tokens and use a model such as google/gemma-4-31B-it. Requests go from your browser straight to the provider; this page stores nothing on a server.
